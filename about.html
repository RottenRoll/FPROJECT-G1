Our Identity

​ RED DENIM is an online product showcase designed to challenge the boundaries of contemporary streetwear. Born from the fusion of utilitarian ruggedness and bold urban design, we curate premium denim collections that celebrate individuality,movement, and structural character.

Project Overview

​This platform was developed as a functional academic web project for ITEC 50B (Bachelor of Science in Computer Science) at Cavite State University - Don Severino delas Alas Campus, Indang, Cavite. Serving as an interactive lookbook, the site features a fully responsive multi-page architecture consisting of a curated Home layout, an expansive Shop grid, this informational About page, and an interactive Contact portal.

Purpose & Target Audience

​Our primary purpose is to provide an immersive, high-contrast digital showcase where visitors can seamlessly explore detailed garment textures, sizing options, and styling concepts. We design our platform for the modern trendsetter—individuals who view everyday fashion as architectural expression and value structural longevity, sustainability, and edge-driven aesthetics.

Design Theme & Features

​Our visual layout strictly mirrors our primary design philosophy: Raw. Edgy. Timeless. ​By combining deep indigo textures with striking scarlet accents and a clean, minimalist grid framework, the platform delivers a balanced presentation where the technical details of the denim take center stage. To elevate the user experience, the interface integrates a dynamic item cart counter and a native JavaScript Dark Mode Toggle, allowing users to smoothly transition the site's theme background to match their ambient lighting preferences.
